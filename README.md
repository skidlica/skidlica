## What I work on

**Use-case design for digital payments**
Merchant-side settlement flows, wallet payments and voucher programs built around stablecoins, written so that operators, not just engineers, can run them.

**Public-sector and government pilots**
Blockchain PoCs with public agencies in Korea, and pilot proposals with overseas governments and enterprises in Central and Southeast Asia.

**Trust and verification for AI agents**
How an AI agent proves it was authorized to pay: delegation limits, signed intent and audit trails that a third party can check.

**Policy and standards**
Following Korea's digital asset legislation, and translating regulatory requirements into product and operating rules.
